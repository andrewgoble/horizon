# Off-theme checklist: brand pieces the theme doesn't control

The new theme restyles the website. Customers also see the brand in places the theme can't reach.
Without this list they'd see the new brand on the site and the old one at checkout or in their inbox.

**Who does these:** Andrew or Jack in Shopify admin and in each app. Claude on the rebrand branch must
**not** change these; it only adds items here.

| # | Item | Where it's changed | Owner | Done |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | Order confirmation, shipping and other customer notification emails | Settings → Notifications → Customize email templates (logo, accent color) | Andrew | [ ] |
| 3 | Klaviyo emails, flows and signup forms | Klaviyo → brand library, templates, forms | Jack | [ ] |
| 4 | Review widget (Junip) colors and fonts | Junip app settings | Andrew | [ ] |
| 5 | Loyalty widget and pages (Rivo) | Rivo app settings | Andrew | [ ] |
| 6 | Chat bubble (Gorgias) | Gorgias → chat widget appearance | Jack | [ ] |

| 8 | Free-gift and upsell pop-ups (Bogos, Selleasy, Alia) | Each app's design settings | Andrew | [ ] |
| 9 | Replo landing pages | Replo editor (they don't use theme styling) | Ricardo | [ ] |
